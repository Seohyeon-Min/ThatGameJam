# Match the director's sketch (`image.png`): the road runs from lower-left to upper-right on screen

## Why

`image.png` shows the screen direction the director wants. The path and the travel arrow run from the lower-left to the upper-right of the screen, at about 24 degrees. In the existing test the road ran from the lower-right to the upper-left.

## What changed

| What | Where |
|------|-------|
| Camera offset `(-8, 10, -8)` → `(6.5, 6, -6.5)`. It looks down from behind-right of the road (+Z). The downward angle dropped from about 42 to about 33 degrees | `Assets/02_Scripts/QuarterViewCamera.cs` |
| Snow ground 30×40 → 120×120. With the lower camera angle the ground's edge showed on screen | `AgentScripts/BuildSamTestScene.cs` |
| Rebuilt the scene with the builder | `Assets/00_Scenes/Sam_Test.unity` |
| Unity re-saved each material's `_Color` to match `_BaseColor` | `Assets/01_Arts/Materials/Test/*.mat` |

## Verification

| What | Result |
|------|--------|
| Captured Play Mode with two offsets | `(9, 7.35, -9)` gives a road slope of about 27 degrees. `(6.5, 6, -6.5)` gives about 25 degrees and fills more of the screen, so it was chosen |
| Captured the rebuilt scene in Play Mode | The road runs from lower-left to upper-right |
| Child position after 9.5 s | z=4.85. Stopping at the obstacle still works |

## Cross-model review

Codex. Correctness, security, and robustness lenses. Scope: staged diff of the two `.cs` files.

No findings. The result was `LGTM - no blockers`. After the review, the comment's "behind-left" was corrected to "behind-right of the road". Behavior is unchanged.

## Problems hit

- During the test, Run In Background was on when the builder called `AssetDatabase.SaveAssets()`. That saved `runInBackground: 1` into `ProjectSettings.asset`. After turning it off, the in-memory value was `False`, but the file stayed. The file was restored from git
- `capture game_view` saves into `Assets/Temp/` even with an absolute path. Each capture was moved out and the folder removed
