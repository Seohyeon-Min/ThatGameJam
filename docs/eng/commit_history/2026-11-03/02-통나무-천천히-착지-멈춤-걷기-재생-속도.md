# Slower on logs, a short stop after hopping down, walk clips paced to the movement speed

## Why

User feedback:

1. The girl's walk animation does not match her movement speed.
2. Her arms do not move when she walks.
3. She should walk more carefully and slowly when crossing a log.
4. She could stop for a moment after coming down off a log, then walk on.

The cause of 1 is in the art notes (`Assets/01_Arts/Models/Girl/README_Animations_KO.md`). The speeds at which the feet do not slide are about 0.1 m/s for Walk and 0.05 m/s for BalanceWalk (model scale 1). The girl in Main is scale 2, so about 0.2 m/s and 0.1 m/s. She walks at 0.9 m/s (`GameTuning.asset`), at the same speed on logs too. The legs move 4 to 9 times slower than the ground.

## What changed

| What | Where |
|------|------|
| A separate movement speed on a log (BalanceWalk). Default 0.45 m/s (half the ground speed) | `GameTuning.babyBalanceWalkSpeed`, `Baby.StepToward` |
| After hopping down from the end of a log onto the ground she stands in `Idle` for 0.8 s, then walks. No stop when stepping onto a log | `GameTuning.babyLandPause`, `Baby.WakeAndWalk` |
| Walk and BalanceWalk play at a rate (`animator.speed`) of actual movement speed ÷ "the speed at which that clip runs at 1x", clamped to 0.5 to 2.5x. The legs follow the walk speed's wobble (±15%) and the slow steps on a log | `GameTuning.babyWalkClipSpeed` (0.9), `babyBalanceClipSpeed` (0.45), `babyClipRateMin`/`Max`, `Baby.StepToward` |
| The rate goes back to 1 when standing (`StandStill`), lying down, falling asleep and being moved asleep, because getting up, lying down and the jump are timed by their clips' lengths | `Baby.StandStill`, `LieDown`, `Sleep`, `RestAt` |

With the defaults, walking on the ground is as before (Walk at 1x at 0.9 m/s). On a log it is 0.45 m/s with BalanceWalk at 1x: the same step tempo as before, half the sliding. `GameTuning.asset` was not changed; the new values use the code defaults.

## Key decisions

- Code cannot remove all of the walk's sliding. To not slide at 0.9 m/s, Walk would have to run about 4.5x, which makes the legs far too fast. So this only adds the link from movement speed to playback rate, with defaults that keep the old look. The real fix is a walk clip with a longer stride (art request).
- The rate is set with `animator.speed`, not a state speed parameter in the Animator controller, so the controller asset is not edited while the Editor is open (`AGENTS.md`).
