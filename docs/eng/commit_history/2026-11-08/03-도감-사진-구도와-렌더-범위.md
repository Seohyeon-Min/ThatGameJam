# Exclude empty particle bounds from encyclopedia photo framing

## Why

The Web Development trace showed an 862ms echo frame: PhotoCamera 853.7ms, opaque rendering 792.1ms. Deeper shader/flush costs are unavailable.

## Cause and change

FrameSubject included empty Ring/Sparkles ParticleSystemRenderer bounds at world origin. With the bird at z239.6 the photo camera moved to (135.79,138.47,-15.51), drawing a wide forest instead of a close-up.

Encyclopedia.cs excludes particle systems with no particles, zero-size bounds and disabled renderers from framing. Live effects and other renderer types remain supported and rendered. The incorrect distant photo becomes the intended close-up. Angle, resolution, far clip and capture timing are unchanged. No constants, startup code, runtime objects or scene/assets were added.
