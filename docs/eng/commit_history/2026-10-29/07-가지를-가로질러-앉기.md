# On a roadside tree branch the bird perches across the branch (ADR 0023)

## Why

The user's instruction: line up with the branch when perching.

## What changed

| What | Where |
|------|-------|
| `Perchable.alignBird`: when on, the bird turns while landing to face along the perch point's forward axis (the way nearer its heading) | `Assets/02_Scripts/Core/Perchable.cs` |
| Slerp from the heading at the start of landing to the target, over the landing time (`birdPerchDuration`) | `Assets/02_Scripts/Bird/BirdController.cs` (`FlyToPerch`, `Land`, `AlongPerch`) |
| Per tree, the way the branch runs out from the trunk (tree 1 −167°, tree 2 −40°, tree 3 65°). The perch point's forward is at a right angle to it. `alignBird` on in the tree prefabs | builder `TreeModels`, `SaveTree`, `Assets/03_Prefabs/Trees` |
| Decision and plan | ADR 0023, `implementation_plan.md` 4.1 |

The branch direction was chosen by drawing both the principal direction of branch-top ray hits around the perch and the direction outward from the trunk. For trees 1 and 2 they agreed; for tree 3 there were few hits and the principal direction was off, so the outward direction is used.

## Verification

| What | Result |
|------|--------|
| `script recompile`, builder `Build` | 0 errors |
| Play: on all three kinds, landing from two directions (6 times) | In every case the bird's forward is at a right angle to the branch (dot with the perch point's forward ±1.00). From the front, its feet hold the branch across it |

## Cross-model review

Codex. Lenses: correctness, security, robustness. Scope: the diff of `Perchable`, `BirdController` and the builder.

| Severity | Finding | Resolution |
|------|------|------|
| warn | With `birdPerchDuration` at 0 or below, the interpolation value becomes 0 or NaN | **Dismissed.** The value is when the toes grip in the landing clip (1.2 s). It is never 0 or below, and code guarding a case that does not happen is not added |
