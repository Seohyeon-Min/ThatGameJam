# Re-place the perch on each of the three tree kinds onto a branch

## Why

The user reported that the bird sometimes perched in mid-air, and asked for the three tree kinds' perches to be placed exactly, so the bird sits on a branch without looking odd.

There were two causes.

- The `tree01` model sits 2.5 m above the origin inside its file (root position 0.086, 2.516, 0.12). When the perches were picked in work record 01, the analysis copy was moved, overwriting that position, so the values were 2.5 m off the tree's space. Tree 1's perch was in mid-air 2.5 m below the real branch
- Trees 2 and 3 used "the vertex farthest from the trunk in a height band", which was the surface at a twig's tip

## What changed

| What | Where |
|------|-------|
| Perches: tree 1 (−0.011, 2.943, −0.310), tree 2 (−0.150, 2.678, 0.180), tree 3 (0.260, 3.924, 0.120), in the tree prefab's space | builder `TreeModels`, `Assets/03_Prefabs/Trees` |

How they were picked: in the tree prefab's space, mesh colliders on the wood, rays cast down every 1 cm to find the tops of branches. Kept only tops facing up (normal y 0.45 or more), with a branch 4 to 30 cm thick, and a small space free of wood for the bird's body (a 0.12 m sphere 0.22 m above the feet). Of those, a spot away from the trunk was picked. Tree 3's branches hug the trunk and grow upward, so only one outer branch at 3.9 m was left.

## Verification

| What | Result |
|------|--------|
| A ray down from 0.3 m above the perch on all 6 prefabs (bare and leafy) | All hit the wood within 0.001 m. The leafy trees have the same wood |
| Play: for each kind, the bird left idle next to it perches; close-ups from four sides | On all three kinds the feet are on the branch. No mid-air perch |
| The game camera's view | The bird on tree 3's outer branch looks natural |

The bird's body (2.2 m wingspan) is big next to the trees (5.5 m), so close up thin branches pass in front of and behind its body. The hidden parts show as the light-blue silhouette.
