# The forest as far as the camera can see, the snow from upwind (ADR 0024)

## Why

The user reported: at the farthest point ahead, the top of the screen has no trees. In the intro, at the lowest or highest point, the snow looks thinner.

## What changed

| What | Where |
|------|-------|
| The forest's left and right ends, behind the start, and past the last stage (`TreesAhead`) reach what the camera can see + 3 m (`ForestPastView`). Trees 471 → 1089 | builder `BuildWorld`, `Forest` (takes the left and right ends) |
| The snow box moved upwind (for the slowest and fastest flakes), plus 8 m both ways along the road (`SnowMoveMargin`). Per square metre 1.5 → 0.9 | builder `Snow`, `SnowPerSquareMetre` |
| Decision and plan | ADR 0024, `implementation_plan.md` 3.3, 4.9, 6.1 |
