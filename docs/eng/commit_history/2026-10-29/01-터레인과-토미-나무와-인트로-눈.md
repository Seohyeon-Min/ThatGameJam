# Merge Tommy's branch; the ground as one terrain, Tommy's trees, the intro stage and its snow (ADR 0018, ADR 0019)

## Why

The director's order (`order_from_director.md`, item 1).

- Merge Tommy's branch
- Replace the floor with a terrain and the trees with Tommy's trees
- Make snow fall in the intro. It stops on entering stage 1. Tie it to the season system
- The floor is one large terrain. Its end must not show when the camera moves. No break in the trees may show either
- The bird cannot go further than about five times the road's width

The user added: treat the intro as one stage, shaped like the sketch (`image.png`). The season has five steps (intro, stages 1 to 3, ending), told apart by the red tint as now. The agent asked 11 questions and the user accepted every proposal.

## Cross-model review

Codex. Lenses: correctness (Unity API, heightmap orientation, camera footprint math, event order, particle Stop/Play), security, robustness. Scope: the diff of the builder and `Assets/02_Scripts`.

| Severity | Finding | Resolution |
|------|------|------|
| warn | `SeasonSnow` does not apply the current season when enabled. If the season changes while it is disabled, snow falls again when it is re-enabled | **Fixed.** `OnEnable` calls `Apply(season.Level)` |
| warn | The view math assumes 16:9. A wider screen may show the terrain's end | **Fixed (comment).** At 21:9 about 6 m more shows; the 12 m margin covers it. Noted in the comment |
| warn | The snow box is also sized for 16:9, so a 21:9 screen has no snow at its edges | **Dismissed.** This project's web setting is the default template (`webGLTemplate: APPLICATION:Default`) at 960 × 600 (`ProjectSettings.asset`), 16:10, narrower than 16:9. Code to resize the box with the screen ratio is machinery not needed now |

A second review run raised only the third finding.
