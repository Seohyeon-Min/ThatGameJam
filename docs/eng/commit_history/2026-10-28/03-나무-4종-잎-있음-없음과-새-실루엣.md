# 4 tree kinds x bare/leafy, trunks with branches, a silhouette for the hidden bird

## Why

The director said the 8 tree kinds are 4 kinds, each bare and with leaves. Stage 1 uses the 4 bare kinds. The trunk should roughly follow Tommy's `tree01` mesh (the user's `tmp/image.png`), with branches forking upward. When the bird sits on a branch hidden by leaves it should still read clearly, as in `tmp/image2.png`.

## Key decisions

- The silhouette shows whenever the bird is hidden, not only while perched, because the camera-side trees also hide the flying bird
- The silhouette is a renderer feature, not a change to the bird's material, so it keeps working when the art's bird model changes. In exchange the two renderer assets (PC, Mobile) change; they are shared team files
- Why the stencil: with depth Greater alone, the bird's back faces draw as a silhouette over its front
- Silhouette colour is sky blue (0.3, 0.85, 1, alpha 0.9), apart from the tree and leaf colours, the snow and the baby's orange
- The bird cannot perch on roadside trees (Q7); not added now
- The leafy prefabs are not used in stage 1; they are for other stages
- The fog map (`Sam_Fog_Test.unity`) is unchanged. When it is rebuilt, its bird goes on the `Bird` layer
