# Refusal when the bird reaches the closed line (ADR 0016)

## Why

The user asked for it. The refusal (landing on a nearby branch and shaking its head) is no longer based on where the click landed, but happens when the bird reaches the refusal line.

## What changed

| What | Where |
|------|-------|
| The past-the-line checks on click and drag are gone. The bird flies toward the clicked spot as usual (clamped to the area and the screen), and refuses when it reaches the line (the fly area's front end) while its target is still past it | `Assets/02_Scripts/Bird/BirdController.cs` (`Fly`, `Refuse`) |
| Refusal happens only while flying, so the perched and landing cases were deleted | `BirdController.cs` (`Refuse`) |
| The decision and dropped alternative | ADR 0016 |
| Bird controls | `implementation_plan.md` 4.1, 6.1 |

## Verification

Done in Play Mode with scripts. Clicks called `Click` through reflection with a real raycast.
