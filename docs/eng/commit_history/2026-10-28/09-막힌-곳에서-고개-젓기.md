# Sent where the way is closed, the bird lands on a nearby branch and shakes its head; in-place flight is Hover (ADR 0011)

## Why

The director asked for it (Bird Boundary Interaction). Instead of an invisible wall, when the bird tries to go where it cannot, it lands on a nearby branch and shakes its head. Once the device is opened it moves as usual. The nearest-branch logic may be reused. The animations are in the zip in the project folder (`BirdUnity-Animations-...zip`). The user also chose to try the new `Bird_Hover` for in-place flight and decide after seeing it.

## What came in

The zip's new `Bird-Animated.fbx` adds two clips to the earlier seven: `Bird_Hover` (1.2 s, loop) and `Bird_Refuse` (0.767 s, once). The README says Refuse starts and ends in the perch idle pose, so it can be slotted in any time while perched. The included `unity_validation.txt` passed with 0 error at the pose seams.

## Verification

Done in Play Mode with scripts. Clicks called `Click` through reflection with a real raycast at the ground. The motions are short, so time was slowed to 0.1 to 0.2.

| What | Result |
|------|--------|
| Import | 9 clips. Hover loops, Refuse once, Landing's and Takeoff's events kept |
| Start | `Hover` in place |
| Click before the limit line (z 12) | Flew there as usual |
