# Join the stages on one road and drop the teleport; the bird goes only as far as the next device (ADR 0010)

## Why

The director asked for it (Seamless World Layout). In one scene the stages' maps join as real ground on one long road, and the game goes on to the next section without a teleport. Each stage stays its own root object. The bird's range is limited to the section and opened when the stage is cleared. The long map the director mentioned in the main scene is not on any branch yet, so, as settled with the user, the stages are joined in `Sam_Test` first and moved later.

| Finding | Resolution |
