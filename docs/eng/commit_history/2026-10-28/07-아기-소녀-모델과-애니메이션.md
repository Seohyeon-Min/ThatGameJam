# The baby uses the art's girl model and animations

## Why

The director asked for it (Implement - Girl Animations). Unzip `NPC-GirlUnity-Animations-...zip` in the project folder, put in the baby model, and add animations that fit its README and the game flow so far. Delete the zip when done.

## What came in

The zip held `NPC-Girl-Animated.fbx` (mesh, rig, 9 clips, 7 embedded textures, blink and flower blend shapes), `clips.json`, and `README_KO.md`. The README in short:

- Clips (30 FPS, all in place): `Girl_Walk` 1.2 s, `Girl_Run` 0.667 s, `Girl_Idle_Breathing` 4 s, `Girl_Idle_LookAround` 8 s, `Girl_Idle_Hum` 6 s, `Girl_Idle_Stretch` 8 s, `Girl_Sleep` 3 s (these loop), `Girl_LieDown` 7.267 s, `Girl_GetUp` 12.567 s
- Flow: Idle/Walk/Run → LieDown → Sleep (loop) → GetUp → Idle. The poses where they join match exactly
- Generic rig, avatar from this model. Forward is Blender +X. No-slide speeds: Walk about 0.1 m/s, Run about 0.59 m/s

## Clip per situation

| Situation | Clip |
|---|---|
| Stage start, asleep | `Girl_Sleep` |
| Waking | `Girl_GetUp` → if the bird woke it, breathing while turning toward the bird; if it woke by itself, `Girl_Idle_LookAround` |
| Walking | `Girl_Walk` |
| Waiting at a device, looking up, waiting for a trail in the fog | Breathing, humming, stretching at random |
| Reaching the sunlit spot | `Girl_LieDown` → `Girl_Sleep` |

## Important decisions

- The model's facing was checked on the rig. The two feet sit side by side along Z and move along X, and the hair is on the +X side of the head, so the face is -X. On screen it also faces the way it walks
- Poses without a clip (crouch, sit and rest, look up) stay as standing and breathing. The grey capsule's tilting stand-ins are gone. The sleeping wiggle that grew with the waiting signals went with them
- `Girl_Run` is not used. The baby only walks
- The walking speed (1.2 m/s) and the walk playback speed were not changed. The feet slide (see Left)
- Getting up plays in full, not cut. It is the length the art made
- The README and `clips.json` were not put in `Assets`. What matters is copied into this record
- `Sam_Fog_Test` was not rebuilt (user's instruction: the fog map is not rebuilt until its basics are decided)

## Left

- The feet slide. The walk clip's stride is small, so its no-slide speed is about 0.1 m/s (0.08 m/s at this size). The baby walks at 1.2 m/s. Either the art makes a walk with a longer stride or the walking speed is decided again
- Each wake-up waits 12.6 s for getting up. Four times adds about 50 s to the playtime
- No clip yet: crouch (rabbits), sit and rest (resting stone), the three waiting signals (toss and turn, rub eyes, stretch), look up, peek, watch
- The baby in `Sam_Fog_Test` is the old capsule and does not work with the current code. It changes when the fog map is rebuilt
