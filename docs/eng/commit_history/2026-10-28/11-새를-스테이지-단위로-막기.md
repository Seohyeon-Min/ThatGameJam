# Limit the bird per stage: only as far as the current stage's sunlit spot (ADR 0012)

## Why

The director asked for it (Bird Boundary Interaction revision). The limit per device becomes a limit per stage. When the bird tries to move past the light pillar that each stage has, it lands on a nearby branch and shakes its head, as now.

## What changed

| What | Where |
|------|-------|
| The forward limit is the Z of the current stage's sunlit spot (the last point of the baby's route). Devices do not hold the bird back | `Assets/02_Scripts/Flow/Stage.cs` (`BirdForwardLimit`), `Assets/02_Scripts/Flow/GameFlow.cs` |
| The per-device limit line (`birdLimit`) and the builder's log limit are gone | `Assets/02_Scripts/Core/PathBlocker.cs`, `AgentScripts/BuildSamTestScene.cs`, `Assets/00_Scenes/Sam_Test.unity` |
