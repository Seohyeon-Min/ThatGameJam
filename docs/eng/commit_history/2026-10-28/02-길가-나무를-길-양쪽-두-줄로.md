# Roadside trees in two rows on both sides of the road; a stage uses only some kinds

## Why

The director asked for trees on the camera side too, even if they hide the road. The user said there were too many trees beyond the road and asked for just a roadside feel, and said a stage need not show every kind; the other kinds are for other stages.
