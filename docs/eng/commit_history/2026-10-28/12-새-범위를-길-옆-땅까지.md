# Widen the bird's area to the terrain beside the road, with more ground and trees (ADR 0013)

## Why

The director asked for it (widen the space the bird may move in). Like the green box in the sketch (`image.png`), widen the space the bird roams. Widen the ground, add trees, and let the bird move between them.

| What | Where |

| Ground and streams widened to x ±22 | Builder |
