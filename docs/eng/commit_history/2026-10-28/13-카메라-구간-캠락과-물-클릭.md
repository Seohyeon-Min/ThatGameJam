# Camera zone: locks on entering the trigger, unlocks on leaving the lock area; clicking water moves the bird (ADR 0014)

## Why

After ADR 0012 limited the bird per stage, the user was asked about the bird stopping silently at z 23 while the log's camera zone was locked. The director answered with a sketch (`2.png`): flying far should unlock it. Entering the trigger locks it, leaving the lock area unlocks it. The areas for going in and going out differ in size. Also, clicking a water spot should move the bird.

## What changed

| What | Where |
|------|-------|
| Camera zone: locks when the bird enters the trigger band, unlocks when it leaves the wider lock area. While the device is closed, entering again locks again. Once the device opens, never. The Scene view draws the trigger (red), lock area (cyan), and camera area (magenta) | `Assets/02_Scripts/Flow/CameraZone.cs` |
| The log's zone: trigger band z 15 to 17, lock area x ±8 and z 13.5 to 21.5 (out to the two roadside tree rows), camera area 6 × 1. Sized so the bird, staying on screen, can still reach every edge of the lock area | `AgentScripts/BuildSamTestScene.cs`, `Assets/00_Scenes/Sam_Test.unity` |
| Water keeps its collider, on the `ClickPlane` layer. Clicking water goes to that spot on its surface. Before, the click hit the click plane 0.7 m below and landed off target | Builder (`Water`) |
| If the first thing hit is an `Interactable` the bird cannot perch on now (such as an off-screen branch's perch area), the ground and click plane are cast again. Before, the bird went to the invisible perch area's position (in the air) | `Assets/02_Scripts/Bird/BirdController.cs` |
| The fog map's zone sizes are written for the new scheme too. The fog map was not rebuilt | Builder |
| The decision and dropped alternatives | ADR 0014 |
| Camera, clicks | `implementation_plan.md` 3.1, 4.1, 4.7, 6.1 |

Done in Play Mode with scripts.

| What | Result |
|------|--------|

| Far to the side (x −20) | Perched on a branch at x −13.9; unlocked |
| Back into the trigger | Locked. Then perching on a roadside branch (x −5.5) kept it locked |
| Far ahead (z 30) | Unlocked at z 24.7 |
| Far right (x 20), back (z 0) | Unlocked both times |

Passing through an off-screen branch's perch area was seen once in the first investigation, but the same case was not recreated after the fix.

## Cross-model review

Codex. Correctness (locking and unlocking, re-locking, click hits), security, and robustness lenses. Scope: the diff of `Assets/02_Scripts` and the builder.

| Finding | Resolution |
|---------|------------|
| warn. The new logic also runs in `Sam_Fog_Test`, which was not rebuilt; the default lock area does not contain that scene's trigger, so it locks and unlocks every frame | Dismissed. The fog map is not rebuilt, by the user's instruction. Its baby is the old capsule, so that scene already does not run with the current code. The builder's fog zone values were updated for the new scheme |
| warn. Only the current position is checked, so at speed 6 a frame over 0.17 s can skip a trigger band 1 deep | Fixed simply. The band is 2 deep now, not skipped by frames up to 0.33 s. A segment test from the last position to the current one was judged not worth the code |

The confirmation review after the fix returned `LGTM - no blockers`.
