# In the fog maze, a call from the bird has the girl run to it, behind her too (ADR 0033)

## Why

The director's order, item 2, point 5 (`order_from_director.md`). The girl rarely follows onto a wrong way, or backward. Checking showed ADR 0028 limited where she could go to the way she is on and the two ways of the fork ahead. The reproduction and the decision are in [ADR 0033](../../decisions/0033-in-the-fog-maze-the-girl-runs-anywhere-the-bird-calls.md).

## What changed

| What | Where |
|------|------|
| On a cry, to the point nearest the bird among all the maze's ways. `Reachable` is gone | `FogMaze.cs` `RunToBird` |
| The way a way leaves from (`Parent`), and `Travel` going back to the way shared with the target's and out to the target way | `FogMaze.cs` |
| At a fork whose dead end's wall she has been to, the right way without waiting | `FogMaze.cs` `WalkOn` |
| 4.12 | `docs/kor/implementation_plan.md`, `docs/eng/implementation_plan.md` |

## Verification

Main scene, Play Mode, stage 3. Woke the girl and let her walk at 3x speed, then moved the bird and cried with `CryListener.Broadcast`. Her position and way number were logged every frame.

| What | Result |
|------|------|
| Before the fix: girl at the third fork, bird over the second dead end behind her | She stood at the fork 3 s, then walked off away from the bird |
| Before the fix: bird over the dead end ahead | She ran into the dead end (fine) |
| After the fix: girl on way 4, bird over the middle of the first dead end (way 7) | Back through way 4 → 3 → 2 → 1 → 0 → 7 to the bird's point (0.0 m). Running at 2.5 m/s, at most 0.01 m off the ways |
| After the fix: called into the first dead end, whose wall she had not been to | 3 s after the bird's point, to the end before the wall (z 120.5) and back |
| Then the second fork (whose dead end's wall she had been to) | Passed in 0.8 s, onto the right way. The third fork (not been there) was 50:50 again |
| Compile, console | 0 errors |
