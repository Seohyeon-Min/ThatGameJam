# Lock the camera in device zones, and wall off the bird until the device is solved (ADR 0007)

## Why

The director wanted the camera fixed per section. By default it follows the bird; when the baby reaches the trigger of a device section, the camera eases over and the range it follows within shrinks. Clearing the section releases it. Trying it in the test scene, the user then decided three more things in turn. The bird also cannot leave the road. The baby-based camera limit goes (the director's request, to show that the bird and the baby move separately). Until a device is solved, an invisible wall stops the bird.

## Key decisions

- At first, the offset a zone applies was delayed with SmoothDamp. The bird is pushed into the screen, and the pushed bird moved the point again, so at the moment of locking the camera overshot and came back (in fog 3-1 it went to z 13.2, then back to z 7). Replaced by a weight per zone that blends
- The wall is not a collider but the Z end of the bird's movement range. The bird does not move by physics

## Cross-model review

Codex. Correctness (order of the camera's LateUpdate and the bird's Update, stage transitions, easing between zones, ranges where min exceeds max, nulls), robustness, and security lenses. Scope: the `.cs` files of the staged diff.

| Finding | Resolution |
|---------|------------|
| blocker. Zones outside the current stage also detect the baby, so triggers of stages using the same coordinates could fire early | Dismissed. Stages are placed apart (plan 3.3). `BirdCamera` reads only the current stage's zones. The fog stage is in another scene for now |
| warn. If a zone is off the road or too far from the wall, the bird's range turns inside out (min > max) | Applied. The placement rule (the zone on the road, its far edge within `birdRange` of the wall) is in a `CameraZone` comment. The three current zones follow it. Not enforced in code: it is a placement mistake, and code that hides it would hide the bad placement |
| warn. `zoneEaseTime` of 0 or less gives NaN | Applied. `[Min(0.01f)]` |

Review rerun after the fixes:

| Finding | Resolution |
|---------|------------|
| warn. The bird's range is set after the bird's Update, so while a zone locks the bird stays outside its range for one frame | Dismissed. While easing, the point moves only a little per frame, so a one-frame lag is not visible. The order has been the same since ADR 0005 |
| warn. min > max (same as above) | Dismissed. Same reason as above |
