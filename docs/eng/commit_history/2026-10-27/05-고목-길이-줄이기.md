## Why

When the user played section 1-2, the fixed camera put the log's top, where the bird must perch, at the upper edge of the screen, so it was awkward to click. The user asked to shorten the log.

| What | Where |

| Rebuilt the scene with the builder | `Assets/00_Scenes/Sam_Test.unity` |
