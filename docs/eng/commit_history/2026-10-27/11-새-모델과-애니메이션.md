## Key decisions

- Glide while moving, flap while hovering in place. Staying in place takes wingbeats, and gliding only works while moving
- The wingbeat during the glide is one Animator state (`GlideFlap`). After the glide clip plays 2 seconds, one wingbeat, then back to the glide. No code change
- The time until the bird counts as perched stays a `GameTuning` value, not an animation event. Tied to the event, a late or interrupted Animator transition would leave the bird stuck landing
- The size was first 0.3 (wingspan about 1.6) but too small from the quarter view, so 0.4 (about 2.2)
