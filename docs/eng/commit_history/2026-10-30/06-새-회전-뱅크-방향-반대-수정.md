# Fixed the bird's turn-bank tilting the wrong way

## Why

The repo owner found this while actually playing: "the bird's left/right tilt angle is backwards! When turning right, the right wing should dip down." It was dipping the other way.

## What changed

| What | Where |
|------|-------|
| In `Face()`'s bank-target calculation, changed `-turnRate` to `turnRate` (dropped the sign flip) | `Assets/02_Scripts/Bird/BirdController.cs` |

## Key decisions

- Didn't reason the sign out on paper — verified it directly in Play Mode. Calling `Bank()` via reflection while paused is a trap: `Time.deltaTime` is 0 then, so `MoveTowards` can't move at all. Instead set the `bankAngle` field and `visualRoot.localRotation` directly to force `+60°`, then confirmed from a camera placed behind the bird (so screen-right matches the bird's own right) that the right side visibly dips. Then called the real `Face(bird.transform.right)` to confirm a rightward turn input actually produces positive `turnRate` / increasing `facingYaw`, and matched the sign to that.
- Left a comment explaining why `turnRate` and `bankAngle` must share the same sign, so this doesn't get flipped back by accident later.

## Verification

| What | Result |
|------|--------|
| Compile `BirdController.cs` | `script recompile` → `completed`, 0 errors |
| Before fixing: forced `bankAngle = +60` directly and captured from behind the bird | Screen-right (= the bird's actual right) visibly dipped down — established that positive `bankAngle` means right-wing-down on this rig |
| After fixing: called the real `Face(bird.transform.right)` 15 times (simulating a rightward turn input) | `facingYaw` rose from 0° to 6° (confirmed turning right), and `bankAngle` came out positive (+7) — "turning right now produces a positive bank (= right wing down)" holds |
| `git status` | Only one line changed in `BirdController.cs` |

## Remaining

- Didn't touch how the bank *feels* in actual mouse-driven play (speed, max angle) — the owner only reported the direction being backwards, not its intensity, so `birdBankSensitivity`/`birdMaxBankAngle`/`birdBankSpeed` were left as-is.
