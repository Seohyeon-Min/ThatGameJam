# Put more distance between the intro's first light and the first log

## Why

Repo owner's request: "let's put some distance between the first light and the log, I don't want the player to see the log [yet]." The gap between where the intro ends (SunlitSpot, the join point with Stage1) and the first log (FallenLog, around Stage1's BabyRoute BalanceBeamStart marker) was only 10 units (to the log's center) / 6.8 units (to the marker).

## What changed

| What | Where |
|------|-------|
| Moved the whole `Intro` object (BabyStart, BirdStart, both BabyRoute points, SunlitSpot, the roadside/terrain tree generators) back by -15 | `Main.unity` |
| Shifted `Intro`'s `Stage.area` (the world-space bounds the bird may fly in) by the same -15 on center.z | `Main.unity` |
| Shifted `Stage1/BabyStart` and `Stage1/BirdStart` by -15 too, to keep matching Intro's new endpoint (used by dev tools like `BackOneStage` and standalone Stage1 testing) | `Main.unity` |

## Key decisions

- Left the log (`FallenLog`) and the stream it bridges (`Stream_1`, part of the shared terrain under `World`) untouched — moving a stream carved into the terrain would mean editing the terrain asset itself, out of scope. Moved the "light" side (the intro-end/Stage1-start join point) back instead.
- Moving the whole `Intro` object as one transform carries everything parented under it along for free. Read `GameFlow.EndStage()` first: stage transitions don't teleport the baby (`Baby.Sleep()` only sets state, the transform stays put) — the baby just continues from wherever the previous stage's route left it. So pushing Intro back is enough to naturally widen the "light to log" gap.
- `Stage.area` is a raw world-space `Bounds` on the component, not derived from a child transform, so it doesn't follow automatically — added the same offset manually. Skipping this would have left the bird's flyable area misaligned with the new road position.
- Couldn't determine exactly how much distance is "enough" to hide it, since the intro's own dedicated camera cut doesn't exist yet (design doc: "cutscene content undetermined"). Went with roughly 2.5x the original gap (6.8–10 → 21.8–25 units) and confirmed it comfortably fits inside the terrain's bounds (z −48.4 to 91.6).

## Verification

| What | Result |
|------|--------|
| Console after the move | 0 errors |
| Watched the full intro in Play Mode (wake → walk → sleep at the light → auto-advance to Stage1 → wake again → start walking) | z progressed smoothly from -37 (new start) to -15 (new join point), every state transition (Waking→Walking→Sleeping→Waking→Walking) fired correctly, 0 errors |
| Screenshot from an oblique side camera | The baby and the distant log appeared to overlap in frame, but that's a perspective artifact of the oblique angle — the real distance was still 25 units (baby at z=-12.6, log at z=10). Couldn't verify from the actual player-facing camera angle since the intro's real fixed camera cut doesn't exist yet |

## Remaining

- Once the intro's dedicated camera cut (design doc §5.2 Phase 2: "camera cuts to a fixed position as the bird nears the baby," content undetermined) is built, re-check from that actual camera whether the log is really out of view. The 2.5x distance is an estimate.
- The intro's own roadside trees (`TreesBehindTheStart`, etc.) could also help block the view, but their density wasn't checked for whether it's enough on its own.
