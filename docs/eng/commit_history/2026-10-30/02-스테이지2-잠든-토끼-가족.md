## Prototype choices and limits

- Mother 1/kits 3, two bypass branches, snowbanks, branch shapes, sounds and reaction timing are first-version values for feedback.
- Branches combine an existing tree model with cylindrical limbs and a snow load; no dedicated branch art exists.
- No baby crouch/peek clip: standing and facing the family stands in. Rabbit yawning/stretching is also unavailable; mother signal is a small sleeping turn.
- Prototype rustle is a synthesized 0.45s, 22,050Hz mono WAV. No rabbit calls or final audio.
- Off-screen demo waits until visible, so it can play after baby reaches the family. It does not block progress.
- Only local snowbanks melt. Global ground snow shaders, flowers, the 1-3 rabbit procession and fog-stage connection remain unimplemented. Fog map was untouched.
- After full `BuildSamTestScene.Build`, run `BuildStage2.Run`. FBX/meta/WAV live in Assets; Temp inputs are only needed for their initial import.
