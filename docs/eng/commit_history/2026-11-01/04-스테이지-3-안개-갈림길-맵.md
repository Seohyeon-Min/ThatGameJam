# Stage 3 fog fork map, first playable (ADR 0027)

## Why

The user gave a new stage 3 map sketch (`20261101_215022.jpg`): 4 forks, walls at the ends of the dead ends, and a small animal at each fork that shows the right way. The girl follows the small animals, not the bird's trail. The decision and the dropped alternatives are in ADR 0027.

## Key decisions

- The sketch has no scale, so start (z 40.5) to goal (z 77.1) is about 37 m. Walking every path is about 46 m.
- Fog density 0.5. At 0.3 it was hardly visible over the snowy ground; at 0.6 the tree bases were clearly washed out. The fog is 1.2 m high, so the girl and the upper trees show above it.
- Each rabbit sits beside its fork, on the side away from the dead end, so the girl does not walk into it.
