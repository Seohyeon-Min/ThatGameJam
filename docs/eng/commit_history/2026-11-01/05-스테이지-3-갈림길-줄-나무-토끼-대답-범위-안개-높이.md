# Stage 3: rows of trees along the paths, rabbits answer only at their fork, taller fog

## Why

User feedback after the first playable. The forks, especially the dead ends with the walls, should be clearer. A rabbit should answer only when the bird cries near its fork. The fog does not look like fog yet and can be taller.

## What changed

| What | Where |
|------|------|
| A rabbit answers only when the bird cries within 3.5 m (flat distance). `CryListener` filters by 3D distance, so `OnCry` checks the flat distance itself, whatever the bird's height (4 m, changed by the wheel) | `FogRabbit.cs` |
| Rows of trees on both sides of every path, 1.9 m from its middle, 1.5 m apart. Dead ends are lined up to the wall. The forest behind is looser: beyond 3.4 m from a path's middle, 2.4 m apart | `BuildStage3.cs` `Trees` |
| Wall trees 3 -> 5, 0.9 m apart | `BuildStage3.cs` `WallTrees` |
| Fog 4 layers (0.15 to 1.2 m, density 0.5) -> 7 layers (0.2 to 2.6 m, density 0.22) | `BuildStage3.cs` `Fog` |
| Implementation plan 4.12 | `docs/eng/implementation_plan.md` |

## Verification

| What | Result |
|------|------|
| Play Mode. Cry 5 m flat and 4 m up from fork 1 | The rabbit stays |
| Cry 2 m flat and 4 m up from the same fork | The rabbit runs. The other forks' rabbits stay |
| Screenshots near forks 1 and 3 | The lower trees are blurred by fog. The rows along the paths show, but the tall trees hide much of the paths on screen |
| Compile | 0 errors |
