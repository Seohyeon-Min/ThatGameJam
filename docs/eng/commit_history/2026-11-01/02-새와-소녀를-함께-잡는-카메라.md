# Camera frames the bird and the girl together when they are close

## Why

The director's order (the context-aware camera in `order_from_director.md`) and the user's talk with the director.

- With the bird alone, the bird sits in the exact middle of the screen.
- With the bird and the girl close, both are on screen. The bird need not be in the middle.
- When the bird flies off, the bird is centred again.
- The camera never moves suddenly.
- Gimmick areas keep today's behaviour (the semi-lock).

The director asked to drop "camera behaviour during the child's interactions" (section 2 of the order). The user chose not to write decisions for this.

## Important decisions

- At first, the framing ended when the bird left the inner 15% margin of the screen. During cutscene cameras and the intro handover, `Camera.main` is not the game camera's view, so the framing flipped on and off every frame (1048 times in one stretch). It now uses a distance limit between the bird and the camera point instead of the screen.
- With the first ease time of 1.2 s, the camera point moved at about 3.4 times the bird's speed (17 m/s) when the framing ended. The bird kept flying off during the change, so the gap between the midpoint and the bird grew. Making the limit to the bird scale with the weight and lengthening the ease to 2 s brought it down to about twice (9 to 11 m/s).
- The Editor's in-memory `GameTuning` kept the defaults of the first compile (1.2 s ease), so they were set back to the code defaults. `GameTuning.asset` was not saved (the last commit's new values are not in the file either).
