# Bird centred on screen, wheel zoom with height and walking, eased takeoff and landing, no landing on water or its banks

## Why

The director's order (`order_from_director.md`).

1. The bird sits above the middle of the screen. Centre it.
2. The wheel zooms in and brings the bird down, and zooms out and takes it up. Walking comes too. There is no walk clip yet, so make it look like walking with another animation.

After seeing it, the director asked for two more things.

- The camera looks unnatural on takeoff and landing. Add an animation curve. This covers coming down to the ground and rising by the wheel, and landing on and leaving a branch.
- The bird must not land on the water or on the ditch between the ground and the water.

The user chose not to write decisions for this.

## Key decisions

- A walking bird that reaches water flies over it instead of stopping at the edge. The user took the proposal.
- It doesn't land on logs or snow banks. At first any `Ground` layer was standable. Then it kept landing in front of the log and taking off again because it couldn't step up, and flying low it went through the log.
- While perched, the camera follows the bird's actual height. The bird moves along the curve, so the camera does too. The user's choice.

## Cross-model review

GPT-6.1 Sol. Correctness (state transitions, null perch, takeoff hold, closed way, snow shelter, intro), security, robustness.

| Severity | Finding | Resolution |
|----------|---------|------------|
| warn | Away from zoom 1, `SetAltitude(y, true)` snaps to a height that ignores the zoom | **Fixed.** Uses the computed height |
| warn | Walking to a branch skips the takeoff hold, so the body moves before the feet let go | **Fixed.** `HoldingForTakeoff` in `FlyToPerch` too |
| warn | Zooming out while walking cancels where it was going | **Fixed.** Taking off from walking keeps the target |
| warn | A failed ground raycast drops the height to 0 | **Fixed.** Uses the current height |
| warn | `Limit` builds the screen patch at the flying height, so a walking bird can cross the edge | **Fixed.** At its actual height |
| warn | It steps straight up onto a tall flat-topped `Ground` collider | **Fixed.** Only terrain is standable |
| warn | Rising by zooming out, it can move into a log lower than its target | **Fixed.** The don't-move-below-the-surface check applies to all flight |
| warn | When the height to clear changes, it jumps without the curve | **Fixed.** While clearing, that height only rises, and each rise restarts the curve |
| warn | Landing checks two points only, so a narrow obstacle makes it land and take off over and over | **Fixed.** Checks every 0.1 m up to 0.5 m ahead |
| warn | On a frame it doesn't move, it lands at the ground height of another point | **Fixed.** Coming down skips the move check, so the ground and the position are the same point |
| warn | Coming from the water, it can land over the bank | **Fixed.** By the same change |
| warn | Obstacles ahead are sampled every 1 m, so a thin one can slip by at a low frame rate | **Fixed.** Every 0.25 m |
| warn | Coming down onto rising ground, it can sink into it | **Fixed.** Coming down, it never goes below the ground |
