# Stage 2 changed to the blocked rabbit burrow (ADR 0036)

## Why

`order_from_director.md` item 1: change stage 2 to match the updated `game_design.md`. `Main` still had the old design (demo bird, snow-laden branches, sleeping rabbit family, bypass). Values the design left open (rabbit counts, burrow look, clearing motion, cry range, waiting signal) were proposed to the user and approved.

## What changed

| What | Where |
|------|-------|
| Blocked burrow device: a cry (within 10 m) makes the burrow rabbits answer and dig from inside; the bird perched in front of the mound pecks and digs. 8 s each, 4 s together, progress kept. Natural 30 s. Once through, the burrow rabbits come out and take the road rabbits in, and the road opens | `Stage2/RabbitBurrow.cs` |
| Rabbits: sit and change idles, hop to given points, sink into the hole and disappear | `Stage2/BurrowRabbit.cs` |
| `rabbitFamilyTime` → `rabbitBurrowTime`, `burrowDigTime` (8), `burrowCryRadius` (10) | `GameTuning.cs` |
| Four old scripts and `SnowBranch.prefab` deleted. `BabyRabbit.prefab` uses `BurrowRabbit` instead of `RabbitActor`. New adult `Rabbit.prefab` | `02_Scripts/Stage2/`, `03_Prefabs/Stage2/` |
| `Stage2/RabbitStage` rebuilt: 3 road rabbits, burrow (hole, entrance snow, sparkle, answer), 2 burrow rabbits, stop at z 51, camera semi-lock | `Main.unity` |
| Builder and Play Mode verification (old `VerifyStage2Waiting.cs` deleted) | `AgentScripts/BuildStage2.cs`, `AgentScripts/VerifyStage2.cs` |
| Docs | `game_design.md` status, chapter 8, chapter 17, `implementation_plan.md` 4.11, 4.17, chapter 6 (kor/eng), ADR 0036 |

Unity re-sorted the objects in `Main.unity` when saving, so the git diff is hundreds of thousands of lines. Compared object by object: 49 added, 82 removed, 5 changed, all in stage 2.
