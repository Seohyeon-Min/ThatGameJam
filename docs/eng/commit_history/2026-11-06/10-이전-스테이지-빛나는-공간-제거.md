# Once the girl has got up in the next stage, the previous stage's sunlit spot goes away

## Why

Item 2 of `order_from_director.md`. When the girl sleeps at a stage's sunlit spot and the next stage starts, once she has got up, the previous stage's sunlit spot is to be removed.

## What changed

| What | Where |
|------|-------|
| `Baby.GotUp` event: when, woken, her get-up clip ends (before looking up or around, and walking) | `Baby.WakeAndWalk` |
| `GameFlow` takes `GotUp` and, unless this is the first stage, calls `FadeOut` on the stage before's sunlit spot (the `SunlitSpot` under that stage's last `route` point) | `GameFlow.OnBabyGotUp`, `SunlitSpotOf` |
| `SunlitSpot.FadeOut(seconds)`: the shafts' strength goes down to 0, then level 0 (renderers off). The ring (`SpringRing`) is left alone. `Show()` undoes it. Disabled mid-fade, it is left as if the fade had finished | `SunlitSpot.cs` |
| `LightBeam.Strength` (0 to 1): scales the shaft, the ground pool and the pool's light on the girl (`_WC_SunPoolColor`) together. The material is shared, so through a `MaterialPropertyBlock` | `LightBeam.cs` |
| `GameTuning.sunlitSpotFadeSeconds` = 1.5 | `GameTuning.cs` |
| Dev tool "Back": turns on again the light at the start (the stage before's) and the end of the stage it goes back to | `GameFlow.BackOneStage` |
| A line in the implementation plan, 4.6 | `implementation_plan.md` (kor/eng) |

"Remove" turns the light off rather than deleting the object: the ring and season effects hang on the same object, and the dev tool Back can bring it back. It fades over 1.5 s instead of going off at once: the girl is standing in that light, and switching it off in one frame jars.

## Verification

| What | Result |
|------|--------|
| Play Mode: intro skipped, dev tool "Next stage", then the wake timer ended at once | After the get-up clip (12.6 s), the intro's light goes to 0 over 1.5 s and turns off. The pool's light on the girl dims with it. Stages 1 and 2 keep theirs |
| Renders from above (before, after) | Before: the shaft and pool; after: none |
| Then dev tool "Back" | The intro's light is on again |
| Console errors | 0 |

## Cross-model review

GPT-6.1 Sol, correctness, security, robustness.

| Severity | Finding | Handling |
|------|------|------|
| warn | The fade only reaches the renderer, so the pool's light on the girl (`PublishPool`) goes off suddenly at the end | **Applied.** The strength moved to `LightBeam.Strength`, which scales the renderer and `_WC_SunPoolColor` together |
| warn | Disabled mid-fade, the coroutine stops and `FadeOut` can never run again | **Applied.** `OnDisable` leaves it as if the fade had finished (strength 1, level 0) |

Not reviewed again after the fixes. The fade and the pool's light were checked again in Play Mode.
