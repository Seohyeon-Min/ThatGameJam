# Easter egg fixes from the director: sizes, rock removed, SoftSnow piles, migrating birds' facing and wing beats

## Why

Item 1 of `order_from_director.md`. The director asked for changes to the size, look and motion of six easter eggs.

## What changed

| What | Where |
|------|-------|
| `HollowSquirrel` scale 2, `CornelianCherry` 2.5, `IcicleXylophone` 1.6, on the prefab root. The squirrel, berries and perch points inside grow with them | `AgentScripts/BuildEasterEggs.cs`, each prefab |
| The placeholder rock under the icicle xylophone is gone. An icicle falls to whatever is under its tip (now the ground). The code is unchanged; only its comments | same, `IcicleXylophone.cs` |
| The snow piles of `SnowWhirl` and `SnowFlower` use `SoftSnow.mat` (the `ThatGameJam/SoftSnow` shader, the snow on the girl). Stage 2's `Snowbank` prefab itself is unchanged | `BuildEasterEggs.Snowbank` |
| The migrating bird models are turned 180°. The model faces -Z (the bird's `visualRoot` is turned 180° for the same reason) | `BuildEasterEggs.MigratingBirds` |
| In flight the flock cross-fades to `GlideFlap` every `flapEvery` (2 s), gliding in between | `MigratingBirds.cs` |
| A separate entry `BuildEasterEggs.BuildPrefabs` rebuilds only the prefabs and does not save the open scene. `Main` was open with unsaved changes | `BuildEasterEggs.cs` |

The instances in `Main` do not override scale, so the scene was not changed.

## Verification

| What | Result |
|------|--------|
| Ran `BuildEasterEggs.BuildPrefabs` | Only the six changed prefabs have a diff; the other three are unchanged |
| Rendered the clearing with a temporary camera | The two trees and the bush are larger, no rock, both snow piles look like SoftSnow |
| Called `MigratingBirds.Begin` directly in Play Mode | The bird model's front (its -Z, turned 180°) matches the flock's heading. The state alternates between `GlideFlap` and `Glide`. 0 console errors |
