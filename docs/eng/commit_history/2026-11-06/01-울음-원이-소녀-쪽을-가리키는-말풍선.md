# With the girl off the screen, the bird's cry ring becomes a speech bubble pointing at her

## Why

The director's request (`order_from_director.md`, drawing `Untitled.png`). From after the intro stage, when the girl is not on the screen and the bird cries with the right button, the white ring points to where she is. The drawing is a speech bubble with one pointed corner.

## What changed

| What | Where |
|------|------|
| With the tail on, it draws a speech bubble: the ring with a triangle added. The tail's point is `_TailTip` (1.45) ring radii out. With the tail off it is the same ring as before | `GlowRing.shader` |
| Each cry decides whether the tail is on: when the current stage is not the first (the intro) and the point 0.6 m above the girl's feet (`girlHeight`) is off the screen. On, the particle's start size grows by `_TailTip` so the ring keeps its size. While the ring shows, the tail turns toward her on the screen | `CryTail.cs` (new) |
| `CryTail.Aim()` before the ring plays | `BirdController.cs` `Cry` |
| `CryTail` on `Ring` | `BirdCryPulse.prefab` |

The tail's direction goes through the renderer's MaterialPropertyBlock. The cry cooldown (0.8 s) is longer than the ring's life (0.6 s), so rings don't overlap. The fog animals' answers use the same prefab but never call `Aim`, so they stay rings. No new texture or material was made.

## Verification

Main scene, Play Mode. The intro skipped; in stage 1 the bird was moved around and the cry played and captured.

| What | Result |
|------|------|
| Girl off the screen, lower left | Speech bubble, tail to the lower left |
| Girl off the screen, lower right | Speech bubble, tail to the lower right |
| Girl on the screen | The same ring as before, size multiplier 1 |
| Intro stage, girl off the screen | No tail |
| Shader compile, script compile, console | 0 errors |

The cry was played by script (`Aim` and the particle), not by a real right-click.
