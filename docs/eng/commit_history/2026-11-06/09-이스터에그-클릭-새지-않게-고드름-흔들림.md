# Easter egg clicks no longer fall through to the ground behind; icicles swing when the bird pecks them

## Why

Item 1 of `order_from_director.md` (updated). Clicking an easter egg also clicked the ground behind it and the bird moved, the icicle xylophone above all. And the icicles no longer fall: the bird goes and pecks one, and it swings where it hangs and rings.

## Cause

When the first thing hit is not a perch it can land on, `BirdController` casts again at the ground and ClickPlane only and goes behind it (the rule for branches off screen and the fallen log). An icicle is an Interactable with no `Perchable`, so the ground behind was clicked every time. So were eggs that can't be landed on just now, such as the flower in the snow once done, or the cornelian cherry with no berries left.

## What changed

| What | Where |
|------|-------|
| `ClickCatcher`: an empty marker component. If the first Interactable collider hit has one above it and is not a perch it can land on, the press does not move the bird | `Core/ClickCatcher.cs`, `BirdController.Update` |
| On the roots of seven easter egg prefabs (not the snow on the bird or the migrating birds) | `BuildEasterEggs.cs` |
| Icicles: falling and growing back are gone. Each icicle has a `Perchable`; its perch point `TapFrom` is under the root (under the swinging icicle, the bird would swing too). When the bird lands it pecks once; at the bottom of the peck (half its time) one note rings and the icicle swings side to side across the view about the top it hangs from. 10°, 5 swings a second, dying away (squared) over 1.5 s. Clicked again while standing there, it pecks again (ignored mid-peck). Disabled, they go back to hanging still | `IcicleXylophone.cs` |
| `TapFrom`: `PeckReach` back along the branch, facing the icicle, so the beak strikes 0.15 m below its top. The branch's way is the level principal axis (PCA) of the tree's surface points near the icicle's top height, outward being away from the trunk. Within 0.8 m of the trunk's axis the bird stands outside, facing in (inside is in the trunk) | `BuildEasterEggs.IcicleXylophone`, `BranchDirection` |

At first a click alone set the icicle swinging and the bird did not move. The user corrected it: the bird goes and pecks it, then the swing and the sound.

## Verification

| What | Result |
|------|--------|
| Edit mode: the bird at each `TapFrom` with pitch -40, rendered | For all eight it stands on the branch with its head just below the icicle's top. The two by the trunk face in from outside |
| Play Mode, virtual mouse: icicle 2 → icicle 2 again → icicle 5 | Flies to 2 and lands; only 2 swings, at the bottom of the peck. Clicked again, it pecks again where it is and 2 swings. Clicking 5 moves it there and only 5 swings |
| The same on the flower in the snow once done (`CanPerch` false) | The bird does not move |
| The same on the flower in the snow not yet done | It flies there and lands, as before |
| Console errors, virtual devices | 0 errors. The virtual mouse was removed |

## Cross-model review

GPT-6.1 Sol, correctness, security, robustness.

| Severity | Finding | Handling |
|------|------|------|
| warn | Disabled mid-swing, the coroutine stops and the icicle stays tilted | **Applied.** `OnDisable` stops the coroutines and puts every icicle back to its place and rotation |
| warn | Builder: with no tree surface points around a branch or near an icicle's top, an empty array throws | **Applied.** The cherry skips that branch; the icicle uses the way out from the trunk |
| warn | If the bird leaves mid-peck, the old icicle still rings | **Applied.** At the bottom of the peck it rings only if the bird is still at that icicle |

## Left

- Moving to another icicle takes the takeoff, the flight and the landing (about 1.2 s), so a quick tune can't be played. The same icicle can be struck again at once.

- Pressing and dragging from an easter egg does not make the bird follow (the press goes to the egg). A hold that starts on the ground is unchanged.
