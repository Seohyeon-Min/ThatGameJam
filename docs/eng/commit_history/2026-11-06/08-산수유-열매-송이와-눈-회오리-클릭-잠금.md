# Cornelian cherry: the bird pecks berry clusters at branch ends; the snow whirl locks clicks

## Why

User request. At the cornelian cherry the bird landed at the middle of the bush's top and didn't look like it was pecking berries on the tree. At the snow whirl, clicking the pile and then other ground cut the interaction off.

## What changed

| What | Where |
|------|-------|
| The berry clusters (`UV Sphere 2`) hang a little in from branch ends, as the model's own cluster does. The top of a cluster's stem (the mesh's highest point, 0.009 from the branch on the model) is put 0.01 above the bottom of the branch there (the lowest point around the branch's middle). At first they were a fixed distance below the branch's middle and some looked to float in the air (the user pointed it out). Each cluster turns about Y around its stem top so its berries hang out along the branch (toward the tip), plus 0, 35, -30, 20, -40, 10° in turn. At first all faced the same way, which looked unnatural (the user pointed it out). The builder finds branch ends in the bush mesh (farthest point in many directions, not the trunk's top) and uses six, the camera's side (+X, -Z) first. Before, four were stuck to the trunk's surface | `BuildEasterEggs.BranchEnds`, `CornelianCherry()` |
| Each cluster has an `EatFrom`: back along the branch, facing the cluster, so at the bottom of a peck the beak is on the cluster's top (`PeckReach` 0.5 m, `PeckDrop` 0.12 m, measured on the bird) | same |
| `CornelianCherry` holds cluster and `EatFrom` pairs. When the bird leaves, the perch point moves to the next cluster's `EatFrom` (moving it while perched would move the bird) | `CornelianCherry.cs` |
| `Perchable.faceForward`: with `alignBird`, the bird faces the perch point's forward itself, not the nearer way along the axis | `Perchable.cs`, `BirdController.AlongPerch` |
| `Perchable.locksClicks`: once clicked, the player's clicks don't count until the bird has landed, as with `PerchOn`. On for the snow whirl. On landing the whirl (Scripted) starts at once, so clicks don't count until it ends | `Perchable.cs`, `BirdController.ClickPerch`, `SnowWhirl()` |
| `BirdController.Perch`: the `Perchable` it is on or heading to | `BirdController.cs` |
| `Peck` was tipping the head back. On this rig a negative pitch tips the beak down (the model faces -Z under `visualRoot`'s 180°). Sign flipped. The ladybug and the flower in the snow are fixed with it | `BirdController.PeckRoutine` |
