# Stage 3 ends at a cliff and a resting stone; the ending plays when the bird reaches the screen's edge (ADR 0029)

## Why

The director's order (`order_from_director.md`). Past the fog maze there is no sunlit spot; the girl goes on to a big stone before a cliff, and when the bird flies out over the cliff near the edge of the screen, the ending plays. There is no ending animation, so the clip of the girl first getting up plays instead. Before the work we asked nine questions and the user answered them. The decisions are in [ADR 0029](../../decisions/0029-a-cliff-and-a-resting-stone-at-the-end-of-the-fog-stage.md).

## Verification

Main scene, Play Mode. The intro was skipped, the dev tool "Next stage" took us to stage 3, and the girl walked the maze alone at 8x speed.

| What | Result |
|------|--------|
| Past the maze | The blocker becomes `CliffEnding`; the girl walks to beside the stone (-1.4, 247.85) and goes to `QuietWait` |
| By the stone, the bird cries beside her (0, 244) and over the maze (0, 200) | She stays standing; 0 animals answered |
| A click on empty air at the top right of the screen (viewport 0.95, 0.9) (calling the bird's `Click` directly; see below) | It hits the click plane (-2.2, 278.6). The camera zone locks, and the bird stops at viewport 0.92. The ending starts: `IntroGetUp` plays, then `Idle` |
| Season | Still 3 after the ending |
| Two clicks low on the screen, toward the maze | The second one releases the camera zone |
| Compile | 0 errors |

A virtual mouse (`InputSystem.AddDevice`) was tried for the clicks, but its position read (0, 0) and the bird flew off elsewhere. So the same ray was cast and `BirdController.Click` was called through reflection. The input code did not change. The virtual mouse was removed, the real mouse enabled again, and Run In Background turned off.

## Cross-model review

GPT-6.1 Sol, correctness, security and robustness lens. Once more after the fixes.

| Severity | Finding | Resolution |
|------|------|------|
| warn | After the ending, going back with the dev tool "Back" and returning leaves `ended` set, so neither the waiting pose nor the ending plays again by the stone | **Fixed.** `BeginWaiting` resets `ended` and stops a leftover coroutine |
| warn | With the camera held elsewhere by a dev tool, a bird off the screen or behind the camera meets the edge condition wrongly | **Fixed.** Only a viewport on the screen (0 to 1, z > 0) counts |
| warn | Using the dev tools F4 or F6 in the last stage advances the season | **Dismissed.** That is the dev tools' existing behaviour. In play the blocker never opens, so the season does not advance |

The second review after the fixes: `LGTM - no blockers`.
