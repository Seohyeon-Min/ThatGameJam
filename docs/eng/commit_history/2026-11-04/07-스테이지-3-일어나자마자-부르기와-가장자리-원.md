# Stage 3: call her as soon as she is up, rings of off-screen animals at the edge, rings yellow over the fog (ADR 0028)

## Why

Four user requests.

- In stage 3 the user wants to call the girl with a right click as soon as she is up, and it didn't work. She woke at stage 2's sunlit spot and reached the maze's `StopPoint` only after walking about 8 m; cries before that were ignored.
- When the answering animal is off the screen, its ring should show at the screen's edge in its direction.
- The ring should show over the fog.
- The ring should be yellow.

## What changed

| What | Where |
|------|-------|
| Where the girl wakes (stage 2's sunlit spot) is the `StopPoint` and the start of the straight way, and `BabyStart` is there too | `AgentScripts/BuildStage3.cs`, `Main.unity` |
| A cry in this stage while she gets up (`Waking`) or walks, before the maze leads her, is kept; when the maze begins she runs to it | `FogMaze.cs` |
| Ring `color` (yellow 1, 0.85, 0.15). When the animal is off the screen or behind the camera, the ring is drawn at the screen's edge in its direction, within 5 m of the camera (`edgeDepth`) so trees don't hide it, at least 6 % of the screen's height (`edgeMinRadius`) | `CryRing.cs` |
| The ring's LineRenderer sorting order is 1, so it draws after the fog (same transparent queue, order 0) | `FogForkAnimal.prefab`, builder |
| Implementation plan 4.12 | `docs/eng/implementation_plan.md` |

## Verification

Main scene, Play Mode.

| What | Result |
|------|--------|
| In stage 3, a cry wakes her; while she gets up (`GetUp`) another cry ahead at (0, 92) | As soon as she is up she runs (`Run`) there, and walks on 3 s later |
| A cry from the start (0, 69); the squirrel is about 30 m ahead, off the screen | A thick yellow ring at the top right edge of the screen |
| The fox on the screen | The yellow ring shows over the fog |
| Compile | 0 errors |

## Cross-model review

GPT-6.1 Sol, Unity lifecycle and state lens. Once more after the fix.

| Severity | Finding | Resolution |
|----------|---------|------------|
| warn | A point in front of the camera but nearer than the near plane is mirrored too, so it shows at the opposite edge | **Fixed.** Mirror only behind the camera (z < 0); clamp the depth to the near plane separately |
| warn | With an orthographic camera the perspective formula gets size and inset wrong | **Dismissed.** The game camera is perspective only (Cinemachine, FOV 40) |

## Still open

- On the screen a small animal's (the squirrel's) ring is small. The minimum size applies only off the screen.
- On the screen the ring is drawn at the animal, so trees in front of it can hide it.
