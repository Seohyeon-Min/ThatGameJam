## Key decisions

- Ways leave a fork in only two directions, screen right and screen up. In the quarter view they always part at a right angle.
- It was first built with ADR 0027's light fog (0.22). In the game view the tree trunks hid the forks. With the fog at 0.9 and only 10 % of that within 2.8 m of a way, the ground and shadows of the ways show.
- The maze trees carry a `WatercolorPaintedModel`, so growing the world forest again painted them too. Only painted trees turn see-through when they hide the bird or the girl.
- Growing the world forest again baked the watercolor of each kind again (`Assets/01_Arts/Baked/Shared/Tree_*`). Those file changes were reverted, not committed. The trees point to the same assets, so they keep the earlier bake.
- The placeholder animals were first too small (squirrel 0.15) for the ring to show. They are now squirrel 0.3 to bear 1.0, with the existing `Test/Animal.mat`. The ring is pulled toward the camera by its radius so the body doesn't hide it.

| Severity | Finding | Resolution |

| blocker | With `wayFogRadius` 0 the fog loop in `Start` never ends | **Fixed.** `Min(0.1f)` |
