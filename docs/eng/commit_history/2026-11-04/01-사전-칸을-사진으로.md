# Encyclopedia slots as photos: a close-up of what was found, an optional photo spot per easter egg, 11 entries

## Why

The director liked the popup and asked for changes to the encyclopedia:

- Remove the text from the encyclopedia. Each slot shows only its number and name on one line, with a photo of what was found below
- The photo is not the full screen but a close-up of the subject. Some surroundings behind it are fine (option (a), chosen by the user)
- The photo is taken when the easter egg shows itself, not when the player clicks. The game (the system) decides the moment
- The photo angle must be adjustable later: a photo spot placed in the scene per easter egg (option (b), chosen by the user)
- The easter egg list went from 13 to 11: 9 Snow on the bird and 13 Feather and baby were dropped. The user edited section 1 of `docs/kor/easter_eggs.md`

## Key decisions

- **Who picks the subject.** The easter egg component passes its own subject (squirrel, trout, ...) at the moment it shows itself. The encyclopedia does not know when to take the photo.
- **Distance.** The first version fit the bounding sphere to the picture height, and the rabbit came out as a small dot. Now the 8 corners of the box are turned into camera space and fit to the picture edges. A skinned mesh's box is bigger than its actual pose, so the sleeping rabbit fills about half the picture height.
- **Photo textures are created at runtime.** One RenderTexture per find. This is creation the design intends (not an object), and they are destroyed with the encyclopedia.

## Cross-model review

GPT-6.1 Sol. Lenses: correctness, security, robustness. Three runs. The third (after the photo spot) only repeated findings already dismissed.

| Severity | Finding | Resolution |
|------|------|------|
| warn | A small subject can be cut by the near clip plane | **Fixed.** The distance has a lower bound that keeps every corner beyond `nearClipPlane` |
| warn | `Release()` alone leaves the RenderTexture objects behind | **Fixed.** Now `Destroy` |
| warn | If `subject` or the camera is null, or the photo fails, the entry stays found without a photo (twice) | **Dismissed.** The subject is the easter egg itself. A mistake should throw visibly |
| warn | If the render request is not supported, an empty photo is kept | **Dismissed.** URP supports `SingleCameraRequest`, and photos were seen being taken in Play Mode |
| warn | `photoSize` can be 0 or negative (twice) | **Dismissed.** It is an inspector setting. The project avoids excessive defensive code |
| warn | Renumbered entries are deleted and recreated, so their GUIDs change (twice) | **Dismissed.** Only the encyclopedia prefab and the test scene, both rebuilt by the builder, reference entries. No real easter egg exists yet |
