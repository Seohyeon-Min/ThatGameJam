# Logo video cut to 4.5 s: clicks open right after the logo is drawn

## Why

The user asked. On the first screen, clicks do not start the game for a while even after the logo is fully shown.

`IntroSequence.WaitForLogo` does not wait a set time. It waits for the video to play to its end (`loopPointReached`); a playback error or `logoMaxSeconds` (15 s) also opens clicks. The video `primavis_anim.webm` was 10.01 s long, but the letters and the dandelion are fully drawn at about 4.2 s, and the remaining ~6 s were an almost still picture. The headphones line shows at 4 s, so clicks stayed blocked for ~6 s after it appeared.

Of three fixes offered, the user chose cutting the video. The `sam` branch rule says no art assets are made on our own, but the user asked for this directly.

## What changed

| What | Where |
|------|-------|
| Kept only the first 4.5 s and re-encoded. VP8, alpha kept (`yuva420p`, `alpha_mode=1`), target 400 kb/s. There was no audio track | `Assets/StreamingAssets/primavis_anim.webm` |

No code or scene changes. Clicks still open at the video's end, so at about 4.5 s (plus the video's load time on the web).
