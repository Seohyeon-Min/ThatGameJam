# The three watercolor textures are baked in the Editor (ADR 0039)

## Why

`WatercolorManager` drew the floor pattern and pencil stroke textures (512, mipmapped) before the scene loaded (`BeforeSceneLoad`), and the brush dab texture on first use (during a cutscene), in C# loops. That takes main-thread time and about 7 MB of temporary arrays grow the web heap. This is item 5 of `order_from_director.md`. The user asked for a separate commit so it can be reverted at once.

## What changed

| What | Where |
|------|-------|
| The drawing code moved into `Make*Texture` functions. In use, the baked asset is taken when there is one; otherwise they are drawn as before. The pencil texture uses the baked one only while the look's five pencil settings match the baked ones. With other settings (tuning a look in the Editor) it draws into a texture of its own, never into the baked asset | `Assets/02_Scripts/Rendering/WatercolorManager.cs` |
| A ScriptableObject holding the baked textures and pencil settings | `Assets/02_Scripts/Rendering/WatercolorTextures.cs` |
| The bake tool. Checks that every `WatercolorLook` shares the pencil settings, saves the textures as sub-assets and marks them not readable | `AgentScripts/BakeWatercolorTextures.cs` |
| The baked asset | `Assets/04_Data/Resources/WatercolorTextures.asset` |

To undo: revert this commit, or just delete the asset. Without the asset they are drawn as before.

## Verification

| What | Result |
|------|--------|
| Bake | The 8 looks share one pencil setting `(0.184, 1.87, 4.03, 0.87, 20.7)`. Floor pattern and pencil: RGBA32, linear, 10 mips, Repeat, Trilinear; brush dabs: RGBA32, linear, no mips, Repeat, Bilinear. Same as the old runtime textures. All not readable |
| Play Mode | The `_WC_FloorPattern` and `_WC_PencilStrokes` globals are the baked asset's |
| Compile | 0 errors |
