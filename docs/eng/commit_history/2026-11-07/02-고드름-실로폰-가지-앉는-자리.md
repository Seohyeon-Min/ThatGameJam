# Icicle xylophone: one perch per branch, turn toward the icicle before pecking (ADR 0038)

## Why

The director asked for it: two branches to sit on, and from one branch the bird taps every icicle under it. The bird should also peck toward that icicle. The old builder should build the same structure.

## What changed

| What | Where |
|------|-------|
| Removed the 8 per-icicle `Perchable`s and `TapFrom`s. Added `Branch_Right` and `Branch_Left` under `Cylinder 8` | `Assets/03_Prefabs/EasterEggs/IcicleXylophone.prefab`, `AgentScripts/IcicleBranchPerches.cs` |
| `Perchable.clickedThrough`: clicking a collider in this list sends the bird to this perch | `Assets/02_Scripts/Core/Perchable.cs`, `BirdController.PerchableAt` |
| `TurnToward` turns the bird on the spot while perched. Landing sets `facingYaw` to the real heading | `Assets/02_Scripts/Bird/BirdController.cs` |
| An icicle on the same branch: the bird turns and pecks it from where it stands. An icicle on the other branch: remembered and tapped on landing | `Assets/02_Scripts/EasterEggs/IcicleXylophone.cs` |
| The builder groups the icicles by the nearest branch mesh and makes one perch per branch | `AgentScripts/BuildEasterEggs.cs` |

## Review

- A null `clickedThrough` would throw → now initialized to `= { }`.
- The bird pecked even if it flew off during the turn → checked again after the turn, and the peck stops if the bird has left.
- `aimed` stays set after the bird goes elsewhere → not changed. Branches have no collider, so landing on a branch takes a click on one of its icicles, and that click overwrites `aimed`.
