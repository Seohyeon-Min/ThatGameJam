# The bird's corridor limit is lifted once it is out of the fog maze

## Why

The user reported something like a wall at the end of the fog stage that the bird could not cross.

The cause was JU's commit `21301bf` (brought in by the JU merge in 06). While Stage3 is the current stage, it pulls the bird's target to within 2.5 m of the maze ways with `FogMaze.ClampToCorridor`. The maze and the cliff are both in Stage3, so the limit also held after the maze. Measured in Play Mode:

| What | Z |
|------|---|
| End of the maze's last way (the exit) | 227.15 |
| Farthest the bird could go | 229.65 |
| Cliff echo `fromZ` | 231.36 |
| Where the ending starts (edge + `pastEdge`) | 254.14 |

So the bird could not follow the girl to the stump, and the cliff echo and the ending could not start. The user asked to lift the limit at the end of the maze.

## What changed

| What | Where |
|------|-------|
| `IsPastExit(point)`: whether a point is further along +Z than the end of the last right way (the exit) | `Assets/02_Scripts/Stage3/FogMaze.cs` |
| No corridor limit when both the bird's position and its target are past the exit. `ClampToFogCorridor` is now an instance method so it can read the bird's position | `Assets/02_Scripts/Bird/BirdController.cs` |

## Key decisions

- It checks the target too, not only the bird. Clicking past the exit from inside the maze, the bird keeps to the corridor up to its end (exit + 2.5 m), then flies free. Clicking into the maze from outside, the target is pulled back into the corridor.

## Cross-model review

Two passes with GPT-6.1 Sol.

- 1st, warn: checking only the bird's position, the limit came back late when clicking into the maze from outside. Fixed by also checking the target.
- 2nd, warn: going back into the maze from beside the exit, the bird flies straight to the pulled-in target and can pass trees near the exit. Dismissed: it only happens in the narrow area by the exit, and the bird does not steer around trees sideways anywhere else either. A waypoint path would be far more code than this case is worth.
