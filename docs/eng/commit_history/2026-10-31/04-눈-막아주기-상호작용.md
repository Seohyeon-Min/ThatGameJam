# Snow shelter interaction (ADR 0025)

## Why

The director's order "Snow Protection Interaction". In the intro, while the girl is held clicked, the bird flies ahead above her at her pace and keeps the snow off with its wings and shadow; she walks looking at the bird. How: [ADR 0025](../../decisions/0025-snow-shelter-holding-the-girl-covers-her.md).

## What changed

| What | Where |
|------|-------|
| Pressing the left button on the walking girl sets `IsSheltering`. The bird flies to 0.6 m ahead above her, then follows at her pace. Ends on release or when she lies down at the light pillar | `BirdController.cs` |
| While covered she turns her head to the bird. Her occasional pauses stay, but she looks at the bird instead of around | `Baby.cs` |
| While the bird is over her, the shadow copy goes toward the sun and the snow block follows her. Both are saved inactive in the scene | `SnowShelter.cs` (new) |
| Stage setting `snowShelter`, tuning `birdShelterAhead` (0.6), `birdShelterCatchUp` (0.5) | `Stage.cs`, `GameTuning.cs` |
| Main: `Intro.snowShelter` on, `SnowShelter` object (shadow copy `ShelterShadow` scale 1.6, snow block `ShelterSnowBlock` 1.8 x 4.8 x 1.8), `World/Snowfall`'s Trigger module kills snow inside the block (scene override; the prefab is unchanged) | `Main.unity` |
| One-time placement script, play test | `AgentScripts/SetupSnowShelter.cs`, `AgentScripts/VerifySnowShelter.cs` |

`GameTuning.asset` is unchanged. The two new values use the code defaults.
