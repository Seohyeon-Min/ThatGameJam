# Move Sam_Test's stages 1 and 2 into the Main scene, make repeated objects prefabs

## Why

The director made `Main` the shared game scene. Main's stage 1 log was the old grey-box version, and after the merge its `LeaningLog` had empty `animator` and `fallClip`, so the fall threw `UnassignedReferenceException` and the baby stopped at z 19. Main had no stage 2 rabbit scene at all.

The director's rules (`AGENTS.md`) also applied: repeated objects are prefabs, scenes are composed and saved in the editor, nothing is created or overridden at runtime. Teammates' newer Main settings (watercolor components, SunlitSpot prefab, snow and lighting, bird altitude 4, baby scale 2) stay as they are.

## Remaining

- Main's baby is scale 2. The stage 2 layout was made for scale 1, so the baby looks as big as a snowbank. Scale is a teammate setting and was not changed. Whether to adjust the layout is a level design call.
- A `BabyRabbit` prefab cannot store its exit points (scene objects). A newly placed kit needs its 2 exit points linked in the scene.
- `Sam_Test` stays as a personal test scene. Future stage 1/2 changes go in Main.
